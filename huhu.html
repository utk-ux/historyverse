ANCIENT WORLD · c. 3100 BC ONWARDS
CHAPTER 01 — THE BEGINNING

Where Civilization Began.

Thousands of years ago, people began building permanent settlements near rivers. These communities developed farming, writing, trade, laws and organized governments.

Ancient Egypt grew along the Nile, Mesopotamia developed between the Tigris and Euphrates, and the Indus Valley civilization built remarkably planned cities.

✧
DID YOU KNOW?

The Indus Valley civilization included cities such as Harappa and Mohenjo-daro, known for their organized streets and drainage systems.

FEATURED STORY · 8 MIN READ

The Rise and Fall of the Roman Empire.

From a city on the Italian peninsula to one of the ancient world's most influential empires, Rome left a lasting legacy in law, engineering, language and government.

The Western Roman Empire fell in 476 AD, but the Eastern Roman Empire continued for nearly another thousand years.

THE ROMAN EMPIRE
HISTORY EXPLAINED

How Rome Changed the World

Ancient Rome expanded across large parts of Europe, North Africa and Western Asia. Its roads helped connect distant places, while aqueducts carried water into cities. Roman law and political ideas influenced later societies.

The western empire weakened over time due to political instability, economic difficulties, military pressures and other factors. In 476 AD, the last western emperor was deposed. Roman traditions, ideas and structures continued to influence later generations.
